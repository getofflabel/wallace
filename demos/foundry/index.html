Behind the drum

Green to bag in nine days

No warehouse, no pallets of stale stock. We buy small lots, roast them the week you order, and taste every single batch before it ships. See the full week

01

Buy small

We work with six farms and one importer we know by name. Lots arrive in 30 kg boxes, not shipping containers.

02

Sample first

Every lot gets a 200 g test roast and a cupping before we commit. About a third of what we taste never makes the list.

03

Roast Thursday

One roast day a week keeps every bag inside a two-day window. Batches are 8 kg, logged by hand, curve by curve.

04

Rest, then ship

Beans rest overnight to let the CO2 settle, get bagged Friday morning, and are on a truck by 2 pm.
