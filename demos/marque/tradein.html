Plan your visit

Fair to ask.

Do I have to buy a car from you?

No. We buy outright at the same number. Plenty of people sell to us and walk away, and that is fine.

I still owe on the loan. Can I trade it?

Yes. We pay your lender directly. If the payoff is bigger than our offer, the difference can roll into the financing on the next car.

What should I bring?

The car, both key fobs, and either the title or your loan details. Service records help the number, so bring what you have.

My car is not a performance or luxury model. Worth a trip?

Bring it anyway. If it is clean and the market wants it, we will still make an offer. The worst case is a free look-over.

How do you pay?

Certified check or bank transfer the day we collect the car. Trade equity moves at the desk the same day you sign.
