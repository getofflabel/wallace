About MARQUE

Nine cars, chosen like we're keeping them.

MARQUE opened in 2016 in Williamsburg with a simple rule: never stock a car we wouldn't drive home. We turn down about nine out of ten cars we're offered, and the ones that make the floor get a 150-point inspection, fresh fluids, and a full history file you can read before you ever ask.

Prices are posted, firm, and checked weekly against the wider market. The showroom is open seven days, and the coffee is better than it needs to be.
