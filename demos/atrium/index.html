The studio

Most studios pick a lane: pilates or weights. We built one room for both, because your body doesn't care which aisle it came from.

Mornings run on the reformers. Evenings add kettlebells, dumbbells and a slow, coached strength hour. Everything is small-group, everything is booked, nobody wanders in mid-class. You'll never share a reformer or wait for a rack.

Your first class

What actually happens

  1. Come 15 minutes early. Someone at the desk sets you up on a reformer and shows you how the springs work. No group orientation, just you.
  2. The class is 50 minutes. Your instructor cues everyone by name, twelve people at most, and adjusts anything that doesn't feel right.
  3. After, nobody sells you anything. You get one email with the schedule. If it clicked, book again. That's the whole pitch.
